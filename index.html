How to play

  • Hero Packs: spend coins to unlock stronger heroes. Pulling a hero again increases their level.
  • Fight Packs: choose a challenge, reveal its monster, and control your active hero in a turn-based battle.
  • During battle: use Attack for reliable damage, Power Strike for risky heavy damage, or Guard for light damage and protection.
  • Winning: earns more coins and stronger loot. Losing still gives basic equipment and a few coins.
  • Fighters: choose which hero enters battles and see their total stats.
  • Collection: track every available hero, with collected heroes shown first.
  • Equipment: equip one sword, one armor, and one shield to each hero. Gear can be moved between heroes.
  • Swords add attack, armor adds health, and shields add defense.
