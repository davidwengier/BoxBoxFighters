How to play

  • Hero Packs: every fighter belongs to one specific pack, and each card lists its fighter chances. Limited rarity has a fixed 0.5% total chance.
  • Fight Packs: choose a challenge, reveal its monster, and control your active hero in a turn-based battle. The free Wilds starter pack can only be claimed once.
  • During battle: use Attack for reliable damage, Power Strike for risky heavy damage, or Guard for light damage and protection.
  • Winning: improves your chances of stronger loot, but every rarity roll can still give Common gear. Limited Arena victories have a fixed 0.5% chance of Limited gear.
  • Fighters: choose which hero enters battles and see their total stats.
  • Collection: track every available hero and every sword, armor, and shield you have discovered.
  • Swords: equip one sword to each hero for bonus attack. Forging keeps your strongest sword, destroys every other sword, and adds part of their combined attack to it.
  • Training Facility: load armor or shields into an open station. A customer pays as soon as they arrive, trains, then returns the gear for reuse.
  • Upgrades: improve customer speed, automatically load gear with Auto Trainer, and increase the chance of better Hero Pack and victory-loot rarities.
